All three families are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, which
permits self-hosting and redistribution with the web pages that use them.

| Family | Designer / foundry | Licence | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Archivo (variable, wdth + wght) | Omnibus-Type | SIL OFL 1.1 | https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Archivo |
| IBM Plex Sans | IBM / Bold Monday | SIL OFL 1.1 | https://fonts.google.com/specimen/IBM+Plex+Sans |
| IBM Plex Mono | IBM / Bold Monday | SIL OFL 1.1 | https://fonts.google.com/specimen/IBM+Plex+Mono |

The `.woff2` files here are the Google Fonts latin and latin-ext subsets, split
by `unicode-range` so an English page never downloads the latin-ext files. The
latin-ext subsets are retained for the future Spanish and Guaraní builds.
